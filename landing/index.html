Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

How frequently are map hazard markers updated?＋

Hazard telemetry and active alert markers update every 60 seconds from satellite telemetry, meteorological alerts, and verified district command reports.

Does the alert system function during cellular internet failure?＋

Yes. Our critical alert engine automatically falls back to cellular SMS broadcasts, which operate across standard 2G/GSM voice channels even during mobile data cutoffs.

Are resource center details confirmed by field teams?＋

Yes. Every shelter, relief depot, and trauma facility is validated with local authorities and designated emergency coordinators.

How do I register an NGO or local hospital on the map?＋

Click "Report Field Incident" or contact our GIS emergency desk below to get verified and listed on the national resource grid.
